⚠ Important information

This is an unofficial dashboard and collection dates or waste types may be incorrect, incomplete or out of date. The dashboard tries to update daily at midnight (UK time), but automatic updates are not guaranteed. It uses a GitHub Actions workflow to retrieve information from UK Bin Day and displays the saved results. The “Last updated” date shows when the data was fetched successfully, not when the council last changed its schedule. Please check your local council’s official information if you're unsure.
